EOS Forms

Quarterly conversation and rock forms. Pick the one you need — each takes you through it step by step, and your answers are saved in this browser until you submit. You'll sign in with your Speckled Trout Microsoft account the first time; the form then sends the email from your own mailbox.
Before your quarterly meeting

5×5×5 Quarterly Conversation — Employee section

Rate yourself on core values, seats, rocks, and the health check, and pick the discussion questions you want to cover. Your supervisor gets your answers by email before you meet.
Start of the quarter

S.M.A.R.T Rock Planner

Define one rock, check that it is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-framed, and map out weekly next steps. Sent to your supervisor and filed in SharePoint.
End of the quarter

Quarterly Rock Completion

Report whether your rock is complete, what you accomplished, and any follow-ups. Sent to your supervisor and filed in SharePoint.

How the 5×5×5 works

  1. The employee completes their section above and submits it.
  2. Their supervisor receives an email with a personal link to the Supervisor review, which shows the employee's answers alongside space for the supervisor's ratings and notes.
  3. When the supervisor submits, the completed review is emailed and filed in SharePoint automatically.

Supervisors: the review page only works from the link in your email. Preview it with sample data.
